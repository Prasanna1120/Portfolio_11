# Prasanna Kumar BM — Personal Portfolio Website

A premium, single-page personal portfolio website designed as a digital CV for **Prasanna Kumar BM**, a Software Engineer and Full Stack Developer based in Ballari, Karnataka, India.

## 🌐 Project Overview

This portfolio showcases professional experience, education, projects, skills, certifications, and achievements with premium animations and a consistent brown & cream color theme.

## ✅ Completed Features

### 9 Full Sections:
1. **Landing Page** — Hero section with name, designations, introduction paragraph, CTA buttons, and animated social icons
2. **About Me** — Professional summary, profile photo, mission/vision 3D flip cards, and "View My Work" navigation buttons
3. **Work Experience** — Pentagon Space training details with animated bullet points and curtain-opening card design
4. **Education** — Two education cards (B.E. and PUC) with hover animations and key highlights
5. **Projects** — YouTube Sentiment Explorer and Shoppie E-commerce Store with outcome boxes and tech pills
6. **Skills** — 6 skill categories with animated progress bars and percentage counters
7. **Certifications** — IJSREM and LARA Technologies certificates with tilt-on-hover and image zoom effects
8. **Achievements** — Stat counters and achievement cards with badges
9. **Contact** — Two-column layout with floating-label form and dark contact info panel

### Animations Implemented:
- Landing: fade-up text, bouncing social icons, floating background shapes
- About: slide-in profile photo, 3D flip cards for mission/vision, staggered buttons with hover underline
- Experience: slide-in cards, staggered bullet points, hover lift with deeper shadow, company badge pulse glow
- Education: drop-from-top and rise-from-bottom cards, cap icon toss on hover
- Projects: outcome boxes pop in sequentially, tech pills elastic bounce, warm glow on hover
- Skills: grid wave fade-in, progress bars animate 0→actual value, percentage counters sync
- Certifications: mouse-follow tilt effect, image zoom on hover, warm cream background shift
- Achievements: spring/bounce zoom-in, badge glow on hover
- Contact: slide-in panels, sequential form underline draw, send button shimmer sweep, contact row slide on hover

### Design & Styling:
- **Color Theme**: Brown (#6b4226) & Cream (#faf5eb) with warm accent tones
- **Font**: Inter (Google Fonts) — weights 300–900
- **Icons**: Font Awesome 6.4
- **Fully responsive**: mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints
- **Smooth scroll**: between all sections
- **Sticky navigation**: with scroll-aware active link highlighting
